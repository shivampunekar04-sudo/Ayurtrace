What regulators currently have:\
● Periodic market sampling and testing (reactive, not preventive) ●
Registration databases of manufacturers (not collectors or aggregators)
● Paper-based compliance filings from manufacturers ● Occasional field
inspections (too infrequent to detect patterns)\
What regulators cannot currently do:\
● See in real time how much of an endangered species is being harvested
this season ● Know which geographic zones are experiencing collection
pressure ● Link a consumer complaint about a product back to a specific
source ● Identify which other products used the same source material as
a flagged batch ● Enforce GACP compliance at the farm or forest level
rather than only at the\
manufacturer

level ● Take preventive action before a species is over-exploited in a
specific region\
